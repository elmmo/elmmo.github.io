About

Hi! I'm Eli. I study human-AI interaction, social computing, and computational social science with the goal of making technology better for people.

I am currently pursuing a PhD at the Georgia Institute of Technology where I am advised by Prof. Munmun de Choudhury and funded by the National Science Foundation (NSF) and Computing Research Association (CRA). Previously, I worked as a software engineer building distributed scalable systems for big data and AI at Expedia, Microsoft, and Garmin. Outside of the lab, I like to dance, cook, and eat 🍽️.
